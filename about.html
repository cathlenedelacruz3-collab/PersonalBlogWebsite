HELLO, I'M CATHLENE

Welcome to my student story.

Hi! I'm Cathlene F. Dela Cruz, and welcome to The Student Edit. I created this blog as a place to share the experiences that come with being a student—the exciting moments, the difficult days, the lessons learned, and the memories worth keeping.

School is more than lectures, quizzes, and assignments. It is also a place where we discover our strengths, build friendships, learn responsibility, and slowly understand what we want for our future.

Through this blog, I hope to share relatable stories and reminders that every student's journey is different, and every small step forward matters.
