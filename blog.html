STUDENT LIFE • NOVEMBER 10, 2026

Finding Balance in a Busy School Week

There are weeks when school feels like a race against the clock. One day is filled with lectures, the next with quizzes, group activities, and projects that all seem to have the same deadline. During these busy times, it is easy to forget that doing our best also means taking care of ourselves.

One habit that helps is writing down tasks and arranging them by priority. Breaking a big project into smaller steps makes it feel less overwhelming. I also try to use short study sessions, take quick breaks, and prepare what I need for the next day. These small routines can make a surprisingly big difference.

Of course, balance does not mean every day will go perfectly. Sometimes plans change, and sometimes we need extra rest. What matters is learning to adjust without giving up. Asking classmates for help, communicating with groupmates, and giving ourselves time to recharge are all part of being a responsible student.

What I learned: Productivity is not about being busy every minute. It is about using our time wisely while making room for rest, relationships, and personal growth.

CAMPUS MEMORIES • NOVEMBER 12, 2026

The People Who Make Campus Feel Like Home

When we think about school, we often remember the subjects we studied and the grades we earned. But some of the strongest memories come from the people we meet along the way. Classmates can become friends, groupmates can become trusted teammates, and familiar faces can make even a long school day feel lighter.

Friendship in school can grow through simple moments: sharing notes when someone is absent, explaining a difficult lesson, laughing during a break, or encouraging one another before a presentation. These small acts remind us that we do not have to face every challenge alone.

Working with different people also teaches patience and understanding. We learn to listen, respect different opinions, divide responsibilities, and celebrate a shared achievement. Not every group task is easy, but each experience can teach us something about cooperation and communication.

What I learned: School is not only a place to prepare for a career. It is also a place to build connections, practice kindness, and create memories that stay with us long after the semester ends.
